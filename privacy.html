Summary: We collect only the information we need to provide our services and respond to your enquiries. We never sell your data. You can request deletion at any time by emailing privacy@evorainteriors.com.

1. Who We Are

Evora Interiors Studio Limited ("Evora", "we", "us", "our") is registered in England and Wales (Company No. 07891234). Our registered address is 14 Portobello Studios, London W11 2DX. We are the data controller for information collected through this website and our services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect your personal information in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data in the following circumstances:

  • Enquiry forms: name, email address, phone number, postcode, project description and budget indication.
  • Newsletter subscription: email address only.
  • Telephone calls: name and contact details provided during the call.
  • Site visits: home address, access instructions and project notes.
  • Cookies and analytics: IP address, browser type, pages visited and session duration (pseudonymised).

We do not collect any special category data (e.g. health, race, religion) and do not process payment card details directly — all payments are handled by Stripe, an industry-certified payment processor.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your data for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Responding to your enquiries and project requests
  • Arranging and confirming site visits and appointments
  • Preparing and sending quotations
  • Delivering contracted painting and wallpaper services
  • Sending our newsletter (with your explicit consent)
  • Improving our website using anonymised analytics
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations

We rely on legitimate interests for enquiry handling and service delivery, and on explicit consent for marketing communications.

4. Sharing Your Personal Data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service providers: email hosting (Fastmail), analytics (Plausible — privacy-first, no personal data), and CRM (HubSpot) — all bound by data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: where required by law, court order or regulatory authority.

All third-party providers are required to maintain appropriate security measures and may not use your data for their own purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary:

  • Enquiry records: 24 months from last contact
  • Client project records: 7 years (legal and warranty obligations)
  • Newsletter subscribers: until unsubscribed
  • Website analytics: 13 months (rolling)

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent for marketing at any time

To exercise any right, email privacy@evorainteriors.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for the website to function (session management, theme preference, RTL preference). We use Plausible Analytics, which is cookieless and GDPR-compliant by design. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Your theme and direction preferences are stored in localStorage, not cookies, and never transmitted to our servers.

8. Contact Us About Privacy

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: privacy@evorainteriors.com
  • Post: Data Protection, Evora Interiors Studio, 14 Portobello Studios, London W11 2DX

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated "last modified" date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance.
