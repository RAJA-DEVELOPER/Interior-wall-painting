1. Definitions

"Client" means the individual or entity commissioning services from Evora. "Project" means the agreed scope of decorating, painting, wallpaper installation or related work as set out in the Quotation. "Quotation" means the written estimate provided by Evora, including scope, price and timeline.

2. Quotations

All quotations are provided free of charge following a site visit. Quotations are valid for 30 days from the date of issue. After this period, we reserve the right to revise pricing to reflect changes in materials costs or availability.

Quotations are based on the scope of work as understood at the time of the site visit. Any changes to scope requested by the Client after quotation will be subject to a written change order and may affect the price and timeline.

Quotations include all labour, standard primer, and the agreed paint or wallpaper materials unless explicitly stated otherwise. Client-supplied materials are not included in our quality guarantee.

3. Acceptance of Quotation

A project is formally accepted when the Client provides written or email confirmation. A deposit invoice will then be raised. Work will not be scheduled until the deposit is received.

4. Payment Terms

  • Deposit: 30% of the project total, due upon acceptance, non-refundable after the project start date.
  • Interim payment: 40% due at project mid-point (for projects exceeding 5 working days).
  • Final payment: 30% due within 7 days of project completion.

Payment is accepted by bank transfer (preferred) or by credit/debit card via our secure payment portal. All prices are exclusive of VAT at the prevailing rate unless stated otherwise. Late payments (beyond 14 days) accrue interest at 8% per annum above the Bank of England base rate.

5. Our Obligations

Evora commits to:

  • Completing all work to the highest professional standard using skilled, experienced craftspeople
  • Using only the materials agreed in the Quotation, or equivalent or superior alternatives with Client approval
  • Protecting all furniture, flooring and fixtures with dust sheets and appropriate coverings
  • Arriving and working within agreed hours, causing minimum disruption
  • Leaving the property clean and tidy at the end of each working day and upon project completion
  • Disposing of all waste materials responsibly and in accordance with applicable regulations

6. Client Obligations

The Client agrees to:

  • Provide safe and unobstructed access to all areas within the agreed scope at the agreed times
  • Remove or securely protect any items of particular value prior to works commencing
  • Ensure surfaces to be painted are free from existing defects beyond normal wear (e.g. damp, structural cracks) unless remediation is included in the scope
  • Make all agreed payments on time
  • Notify Evora promptly of any concerns arising during the works

Evora's total liability for any claim arising from a project shall not exceed the total project value paid by the Client. We are not liable for indirect, consequential or special damages including loss of enjoyment or rental income.

We hold Public Liability Insurance to £5,000,000 and Employer's Liability Insurance to £10,000,000. Evidence of insurance is available upon request.

8. Craftsmanship Warranty

All Evora projects are covered by a 2-year craftsmanship warranty (3 years for Estate package clients). Under this warranty, we will return and rectify — at no cost to the Client — any defect in the paintwork or wallpaper installation that is attributable to our workmanship.

The warranty does not cover: damage caused by the Client or third parties; normal wear and tear; changes in humidity causing wallpaper to lift at edges in rooms with inadequate ventilation; fading of paint or wallpaper due to sunlight; or defects in client-supplied materials.

9. Cancellation Policy

Evora may suspend or cancel a project if site conditions are unsafe, if Client obligations are not met, or in circumstances of Force Majeure. In such cases, the Client will be charged only for work completed.
