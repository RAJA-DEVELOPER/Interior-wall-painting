Our Story · Est. 2009

Fifteen Years of Painting with Purpose

Evora began in 2009 as an artisan studio in West London. Today we are a collective of 12 master craftspeople, united by an obsession with surface, colour and the invisible craft that transforms living spaces into timeless homes.

Philosophy

Three Principles That Guide Every Brushstroke

01

Colour as Emotion

We believe colour is the single most powerful design tool in any interior. The right shade can enlarge a room, calm a mind or energise a morning. We treat colour selection as a deeply considered, personal process — never prescriptive.

Colour Consulting
02

Craft Without Compromise

Every Evora painter has completed a minimum five-year formal apprenticeship. We prepare surfaces properly, use only premium materials and never rush. The result is a finish that outlasts trends and withstands time.

Our Services
03

Care That Goes Beyond Paint

We treat your home with the same respect we'd treat our own. Every surface protected. Every piece of furniture moved with care. Every project left spotless — and backed by a two-year guarantee.
